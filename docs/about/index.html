ABOUT

Learn AI until you can explain it.

A free site for learning AI and machine learning until you can explain it, with interactive lessons and interview practice.

Why this site exists

You can read about an idea, recognise it and still get stuck when someone asks you to explain it. AIML Rounds is built to close that gap. Its 311 lessons cover maths, machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, AI engineering and system design.

How lessons work

  • It starts with a question. Each lesson opens with what an interviewer would actually ask.
  • You build the idea. One analogy, one picture, then a lab you can try to break.
  • You say it out loud. Lessons end with you explaining the answer and taking follow-up questions, because reading isn't the same as knowing.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every lesson is free to read, and you don't need an account.

Who is it for?

Anyone preparing for AI or ML interviews, and engineers who want to understand these ideas properly. You can start from zero, or jump straight to the part you need.

Where should I start?

If you're new, begin with Fundamentals. If you have an interview soon, go to the interview questions and fill the gaps they show. If you already build systems, try System Design.

How much maths do I need?

Less than you might expect. Fundamentals teaches the maths as it's used in ML, and each lesson lists what to read first. Nothing is locked, so you can skip what you know.

Will I learn to code models here?

Lessons focus on how ideas work and how to explain them, so they have very little code. They pair well with a hands-on course or your own projects.

Are the numbers from real companies?

Where a lesson uses a real system, it links to its sources. Numbers in labs and estimates are examples for practice, unless the lesson says otherwise.

Does it save my progress?

Yes, in your own browser. It isn't sent anywhere, so it won't follow you to another device. See the privacy note.

I found a mistake. What should I do?

Please email me with the lesson's name. Fixes go in as soon as they can.
