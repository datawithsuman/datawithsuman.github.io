PRIVACY

Privacy, in plain words.

What this site stores, and which outside services your browser connects to.

What the site itself keeps

  • There are no accounts, sign-ups, ads or analytics.
  • Your lesson progress and which sections you've opened are saved in your own browser, using local storage. They aren't sent to the site. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Outside services

Like most websites, pages load through other services, and these can see basic request details such as your IP address:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.
  • cdnjs, from Cloudflare, provides the maths display on lesson pages.
  • Links to other sites follow those sites' own policies once you click them.
